03 - Automated evidence

A focused regression check turns the most expensive risk into a repeatable gate.

upgrade-plan.spec.tsPlaywright
test('upgraded account shows Pro plan after payment confirmation', async ({ page }) => {
  await page.goto('/billing');
  await page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Upgrade to Pro' }).click();
  await completeTestPayment(page);
  await expect(page.getByText('Payment confirmed')).toBeVisible();
  await page.reload();
  await expect(page.getByTestId('current-plan')).toHaveText('Pro');
});
